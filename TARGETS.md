# Initial Targets

These are target categories, not instructions to spam public threads.

## Best Buyers

- Bounty hunters attempting Algora issues with repeated collisions.
- Maintainers of repos with 3+ open bounty PRs on the same issue.
- Developers with stale bounty PRs and no clear CI diagnosis.
- Agent builders who need a feed of attemptable tasks.

## Good First Outreach Contexts

- Threads where maintainers complain about duplicate AI PRs.
- Discord/Slack channels that explicitly allow bounty tooling or services.
- Developers who already ask "is this still open?" on bounty issues.
- PRs that are stale, merge-clean, and tied to an open bounty.

## Avoid

- Commenting unsolicited offers on quiet issue threads.
- Pitching Opire-only bounty hunters until account setup is clear.
- Claiming that a scan guarantees payment.
- Sending generic bulk messages.
